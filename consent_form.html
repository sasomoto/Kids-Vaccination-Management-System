Consent Form

I, the undersigned, being the parent/legal guardian of the above-named child, confirm that:

  1. Information Provided: I have been provided with detailed information about the vaccine, including its purpose, potential benefits, risks, and possible side effects.
  2. Opportunity for Questions: I have had the opportunity to ask questions about the vaccine and have received answers to my satisfaction.
  3. Voluntary Consent: I understand that vaccination is voluntary and that I can refuse or withdraw consent at any time before the vaccine is administered.
  4. Health Status: I have disclosed all relevant health conditions and allergies of the child to the healthcare provider.
  5. Agreement to Vaccination: I consent to the administration of the vaccine to my child as listed above.
  6. Liability: I understand that the healthcare provider administering the vaccine is acting in accordance with standard medical practice and regulations.
